# Fleur de sel — Research & Plan

> **Fleur de sel** (also written "Fleur de Sel" and "Boulangerie Fleur de sel"; the team signs "L'équipe Fleur de sel" and shortens it to **"FDS"**) is a young artisan **boulangerie-viennoiserie** at **161 rue de Saint-Malo, 35000 Rennes**. It sits on the corner of boulevard de Verdun in the **Saint-Martin** quartier and opened at 6:30 on **Tuesday 8 July 2025**. Two croissant champions run it. **Baptiste Cortais** is the *chef tourier* and won the 1st **Concours National du Meilleur Croissant au Beurre** in 2019. **Mathis Ruelleux** is the baker and won **Meilleur Croissant au Beurre de Bretagne 2022**. They met at Maison Fraudin in Saint-Grégoire and bought the old Maison Poirier bakery. They reset it as a white, minimal shop with a gold logo, which is a graphic croissant. Their idea is **« décliner la pâtisserie en viennoiserie »**: there is no classic entremets, and every pastry is rebuilt in croissant or brioche dough. Their signature is a **bar à flans** on a crisp croissant-dough base with Madagascar-vanilla custard. It has 7 flavours a day that change with the seasons, at **€3.90 a slice**. Customers cross Rennes for it, and the tourist office lists it among the best flans in the city. The bread is **organic sourdough** (Agence Bio certified, 15 h fermentation), except one conventional baguette tradition. It includes a Breton **bara** made with buckwheat and Guérande salt. The butter is **AOP Charentes-Poitou** butter. They use no margarine, keep a short "ADN clair" range and "prix raisonnables", and open early six days a week, Sundays included. Their tagline is **« L'or du four, le sel du goût. »** They chose the name because salt is what gives everything its taste: « sans le sel, il n'y a pas de goût, il n'y a rien ».

## 6. Place & context

- **Quartier Saint-Martin** (Nord – Saint-Martin): a former industrial faubourg grown around the **Pont Saint-Martin** and the **Moulins Saint-Martin** on the Ille. It was reshaped by the **Canal d'Ille-et-Rance (1804–1832)**. It has about 5,700 residents on 1.76 km², an average age of 28, nearly 60% single-person households and 73% flats (https://edito.seloger.com/node/21514).
- **The corner:** rue de Saint-Malo × boulevard de Verdun, at the quiet residential **north end** of rue de Saint-Malo, about 1 km from the nightlife south end at Place Sainte-Anne. Nearby streets: rue de Coëtlogon, rue Joseph Lotte, rue Lenoir, rue Charles Laurent (OSM). In 1792 the street was called "rue Port-Malo" (Wikisource 1883).

## 9. Design direction

**Skill reasoning used:**
- `ui-ux-pro-max --design-system` (variance 7, motion 9, density 3) returned:
  - **Kept:** the "Scroll-Triggered Storytelling" pattern (chapters, each fully readable without scroll effects), the **premium black + gold accent** colour logic, and a Flip page-transition preset.
  - **Rejected:** its "Liquid Glass" style (Apple system chrome, wrong for a bakery) and its Cormorant + Montserrat pairing (a fashion/jewellery register).
- `design-taste-frontend`:
  - **Design read:** *a showcase site for a design-literate Rennes neighbourhood and flan-hunting audience, with a white-and-gold, épuré, dawn-craft language, leaning toward native CSS tokens + Tailwind v4 + GSAP ScrollTrigger/Lenis in vanilla TS.*
  - **Dials:** VARIANCE 7 / MOTION 8 / DENSITY 3.
  - Its palette ban on cream, brass and espresso applies, so we use a **cool salt-white and true gold**. This is justified because white and gold is the real shopfront.
  - It also governs: no em-dashes in site copy, max one marquee, max 1 eyebrow per 3 sections, one radius system, no custom cursor, real-quote-only testimonials.

### Concept: **« Tourage »**: *De 1 h du matin à 6 h 30, l'or se plie dans le sel.*
**The idea:** the website is laminated like their dough. Gold layers (butter, *l'or du four*) and salt-white layers (dough and fleur de sel, *le sel du goût*) fold over each other as you scroll. The site opens in pre-dawn ink, when the bakers start, and "bakes" to the white-and-gold shop at 6:30. This is grounded in four real facts:
- Their chef tourier and the « croissant en fil rouge ».
- Their tagline.
- Their own pre-dawn photo narrative (« il commence bien avant l'aube 🌙 »).
- The white-and-gold shopfront.

**Personality keywords:** précis, lumineux, épuré, généreux, de quartier, champion sans frime, salé-sucré.

### Palette (our proposal; brand only says white + gold)

**Theme lock:**
- The page is **salt-white light** after the hero. The hero's night-to-day transition is the **one** allowed theme switch, a story device.
- Dark mode (`prefers-color-scheme: dark`) maps salt to night and ink to salt, and keeps gold as the accent.
- **Radius system:** sharp (0) for layout and images; **pill** for buttons and flavour chips. That is the rule, everywhere.

### Typography
- **Display: Schibsted Grotesk** (variable, 500–900, with italic). An *épuré, très moderne* grotesk to match the shopfront, not a "luxury serif" cliché. Tight tracking on big numbers (2019, 2022, 6:30, 3,90 €).
- **Body: Hanken Grotesk** (400–600), readable at 16–18px.
- **Labels, prices and hours: Geist Mono** (400–500), which echoes their Instagram **price-tag product cards** ("notre best seller 3.90€"). Use it in the mono price tag, the hours table and the « 15 h » counters.
- Self-host with @fontsource packages (check the exact npm names first). No Google Fonts `<link>`. `font-display: swap`.
- French typography: a non-breaking narrow space before `: ; ! ?`, « guillemets », prices as `3,90 €`, hours as `6 h 30`.

### Imagery approach (we may NOT use their photos)
- No scraped Instagram, @studio.jacobson or tourist-office (Betty Lautredou) photos. Their rights are not ours.
- **Original art only:**
  1. **SVG lamination cross-sections**: alternating gold and salt strata, honeycomb crumb cells (procedural Voronoi via a small TS generator).
  2. **Flan slices as SVG geometry**: a 6-slice disc, a croissant-dough rim with strata, a custard face in the flavour chip colour with a topping glyph.
  3. **Fleur de sel crystals**: small canvas or SVG pyramidal crystals with a gold rim-light.
  4. **A night-to-dawn gradient sky** made with `@property` animated stops.
  5. **Line-art products**: croissant, bara, éclair, religieuse, burger, in a 1.5px gold stroke. Original drawings, not their logo.
  6. **WebGL butter-sheen shader** (optional, lazy-loaded) on the hero croissant line, with a CSS fallback.
- Mark every spot where a real photo would be better as `<!-- TODO photo réelle, avec autorisation -->`. No image-generation tool is available in this environment.

### Layout ideas
- **Header:** a 64px bar with a text wordmark (we do not have the logo; use "Fleur de sel" in Schibsted 700 with a gold dot). Navigation: Boutique · Bar à flans · Savoir-faire · Maison · Journal · Infos. The header shows **live open/closed status** computed from the real hours (« Ouvert jusqu'à 19 h » / « Fermé le lundi »).
- **Home chapters**, each a different layout family:
  1. Pinned dawn hero.
  2. Pinned lamination stack.
  3. Horizontal-pan flan bar.
  4. Asymmetric "two champions" split with giant mono years.
  5. A bento of the « ADN clair » range, with exact cell count.
  6. A single marquee of real customer words (the only marquee on the site).
  7. Ingredient map / « Le quartier » with a schist trace line.
  8. Hours and access as a big mono price-tag board.
- **Product cards are "étiquettes"**: white tag, mono price, a hole-punch circle, tilted ±1.5°.
- A **timber-frame (pans de bois) grid** is the underlying 12-column grid, revealed as faint diagonal hairlines only on the Maison page.

### Signature interaction: **« Le Bar à flans »**
- A large SVG flan disc of **6 slices** (their real format).
- **Desktop:** drag or rotate it. **Mobile:** swipe, with buttons as an alternative.
- The slice facing the viewer **lifts out** (GSAP Flip / transform), shows its **laminated croissant-dough base** strata, and a mono price tag flips in: « Pistache · 3,90 € la part ».
- A season toggle (Printemps / Été / Automne / Hiver) re-fills the disc with that season's 7-flavour board (mock rotation, built from real flavour names).
- It ends with « Les 7 parfums du jour sont sur notre Instagram » and an « Appeler la boutique » tel link. **No ordering flow** (none exists).
- **Secondary micro-signature, « une pincée de fleur de sel »:** on primary CTA press and on adding a flan to "favoris", 6–10 salt crystals spring out with physics and fade in under 500ms (Bernice-crumbs logic, our salt).

### Grounding added in the critic pass
- **The dawn concept is their own idea, twice over:** « Un croissant, ça se mérite… il commence bien avant l'aube » (2025 reel) and « On vous attend dimanche, dès l'aube. 🤍 » (Sep 2026). Keep « Avant l'aube » as the hero; it is not our invention.
- **Section headlines use their contrast pattern** (short setup, then the product): e.g. the bread chapter opens with their real line « Il y a les grands discours. Et puis il y a le pain. »; the flan bar with « Celui qui met tout le monde d'accord. » (on the framboise card). Pull from `voice.ts`, not from invented slogans.
- **Their emoji palette maps onto ours:** ✨ (gold glint), 🌙 (night hero), 🤍 (salt-white). Do not use emoji in site copy; translate them into a gold glint, the moon-to-dawn clock and the salt-white ground.
- **The flan bar has a real community loop** (comment contest → limited edition). The disc gets a fourth ring state, « Édition limitée », with a gold outline chip, used for the 4 real limited editions.
- **Tone guard:** the CM's playful first person (« le CM ») belongs on Instagram. The site speaks as « nous / l'équipe Fleur de sel ».

### Motion language
- **Libraries:** GSAP + ScrollTrigger + SplitText + Flip (GSAP plugins are now free; confirm the current licence and docs), Lenis smooth scroll, CSS scroll-driven animations (`animation-timeline: view()`) for light reveals, and the **View Transitions API** (`@view-transition { navigation: auto; }`) for cross-page transitions in the Vite multi-page build. Check caniuse and use progressive enhancement.
- Animate only `transform`, `opacity` and `clip-path`. No `window` scroll listeners.
- **Hero, « Avant l'aube »:**
  - The page loads in `--night`. A mono clock reads **01:00** (their real opening-day start).
  - Scroll is scrubbed. The clock runs to **06:30**. A gold line draws an original croissant, stroke by stroke. Salt crystals drift upward like flour in light.
  - The sky gradient (`@property` stops) warms from night to gold to salt-white.
  - At 06:30 a clip-path "door" opens onto the white shop chapter. H1: **« L'or du four, le sel du goût. »** CTAs: « Voir le bar à flans » and « Horaires & accès ».
  - On phones the clock auto-plays once (≈2.4 s) instead of a long pin.
- **Scroll storytelling:**
  - **Tourage**: a pinned section where gold and salt strata stack as you scroll, simple fold then double fold. SplitText reveals « décliner la pâtisserie en viennoiserie ». The strata then morph into cross-sections of croissant, éclair, religieuse and flan.
  - **Bar à flans**: a horizontal pan through the 7 flavours of the day.
  - **Deux croissants, deux titres**: the years **2019** and **2022** count up in mono, and a gold rule draws between them.
  - **15 h**: a fermentation timeline scrub for the bread.
  - **Le quartier**: an SVG path draws from the canal and the Prairies Saint-Martin, past the Église Saint-Martin, to the corner of rue de Saint-Malo and boulevard de Verdun.
- **Transitions:** View Transitions between pages, with a **salt-to-gold wipe** (a horizontal stratum slides like a sheet of dough). Products shared between list and detail morph with `view-transition-name`. Exits are faster than entries (≈240ms vs 420ms), easing `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`.
- **Micro-interactions:**
  - A gold hairline underline draws on link hover.
  - Price tags swing slightly on hover (spring).
  - The open/closed status pill crossfades.
  - Flavour chips press to `scale(.97)`.
  - The salt-crystal burst on CTAs.
  - Filters reflow with Flip in the catalogue.
  - Tap targets ≥ 44px.
- **Reduced-motion fallback (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`):**
  - No Lenis, no pin, no scrub, no particles.
  - The hero renders in its final **6:30 daylight state** with the croissant line fully drawn.
  - The lamination section shows the final stacked strata as a static figure.
  - The flan disc becomes a static **grid of 7 slice cards** with season tabs.
  - Page transitions become a 150ms crossfade.
  - Every chapter stays fully readable in DOM order.
- Low-power and mobile devices also drop the WebGL sheen and reduce particle counts.
